>> So I've been thinking a lot about consistency lately. The thing nobody tells you is that motivation is unreliable. You wake up some days and you just do not feel it. And if your whole system depends on feeling motivated, your system will fail. What actually works is reducing the activation energy for the thing you want to do. Put your running shoes by the door. Keep the guitar on a stand, not in the case. Make the good behavior the path of least resistance. And on the days you really do not want to, lower the bar instead of skipping. Do five minutes. Five minutes counts. The streak matters more than the intensity because the streak is what builds the identity. Once you see yourself as someone who shows up, the showing up gets easier. That is the whole game really. It is not about a perfect plan. It is about a plan you will actually follow on your worst day.
