# Best Contact Method

The owner problem is real: most table rows have `decision_maker=unknown`. The solution is a routing
ladder, not a single channel.

## Recommended Ladder

1. **Direct email first** when Apify found an email. It is least intrusive, easy to forward internally,
   and works well for AU properties.
2. **Instagram DM second** when the property feed is active. It is native to the offer, because the
   pitch is about social content and the preview can be watched in-channel.
3. **Facebook DM for Thailand** when the page is active or currently running ads. Many TH operators
   still treat FB/LINE as commercial surfaces.
4. **Phone as owner-discovery**, not the pitch. Ask: "Who is the best person for marketing/content?"
5. **LinkedIn only after enrichment** finds a real owner, GM, marketing manager, or hospitality group
   operator.

## How To Reach The Owner

Do not need the owner for the first touch if the official property inbox is small/owner-operated.
Use the first message to earn routing:

```
Quick one. Is there a better person who handles marketing/content for <property>?
I made a short preview from your public photos and wanted to send it to the right person.
```

For T1s, enrich owner/operator before sending:
- Google: `<property> owner`, `<property> founder`, `<property> general manager`
- LinkedIn: property/company plus "Owner", "Founder", "General Manager", "Marketing"
- About page and press pages
- Booking engine footer and email domain
- Facebook posts naming management/owners

## What Not To Do

- Do not blast the same static critique to all leads.
- Do not say "your creative is not good enough."
- Do not make full custom reels for every cold lead before they opt in.
- Do not promise bookings, direct revenue, or ad performance before instrumentation.
- Do not ask for a Zoom call as the first CTA. The async preview is the wedge.
