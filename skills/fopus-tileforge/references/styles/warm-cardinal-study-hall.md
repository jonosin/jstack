Resolve these paths against the active repository. Record every input role and hash.
Never infer geometry, collision, seating, or occupancy from the populated image; it contains the known upper-tabletop character-placement defect. Keep base environment art character-free. Place characters only through validated authored seat or spawn anchors.

## Geometry

- Logical tile: 24×24 pixels.
- View: top-down three-quarter orthographic on a square grid.
- Character compatibility: 32×40 student, about 1.67 tile heights.
- Seating directions: `up`, `down`, `left`, `right` only.
- Object source cells: 96×96 pixels unless the selected contract states otherwise.
- Transparent-object chroma: `#ff00ff`; tolerance 128.

## Visual language

Use crisp pixel clusters, broad shapes, modest dark-brown outlines, restrained wooden texture, limited highlights, and simple reusable silhouettes. Create richness through layered shelves, books, plants, rugs, lamps, and personal objects rather than micro-texture.

Use warm walnut, honey wood, burgundy, moss, muted navy, cream, and cool dusk blue. Keep local lamps amber and ambience cool. Light overlays remain reusable and separate from floor, collision, and seat data.

## Composition and scale

Prefer broad central circulation, two-to-four-tile clearances, complete room edges, distinct communal/solo/booth/lounge/refreshment zones, and a mix of occupied and empty seats. Characters remain visually subordinate to the room.

## Avoid

No isometric diamond grid, diagonal furniture, angled chairs, diagonal seated poses, free rotation, painterly texture, smooth 3D rendering, soft vector edges, tiny illegible details, labels, UI, logos, brands, advertising, or baked collision cues.

## QA

Accept only when individual assets remain simple, assembled interiors feel detailed through layering, the room reads spacious, all seats are unambiguously cardinal, warm local light contrasts with cool ambience, and the accepted student sprite matches the asset scale.
